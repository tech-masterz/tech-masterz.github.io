Privacy Policy for Sniper

Last updated: 2026-10-20

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Sniper (VR Simulation Game), a virtual reality sniper game. By using the game, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Data Collection

Sniper VR may access basic Meta user information, such as your user ID, username, display name, and avatar, as well as your game score, for leaderboard functionality.

This information is used only to support leaderboard features. The game does not require an internet connection to function, except for features that require online access, such as retrieving or updating leaderboard information.

2. Data Use

Information such as your user ID, username, and score is used to identify players, rank their scores, and display standings on the in-game leaderboard.

We do not use this information for advertising, marketing, or unrelated purposes. We do not independently collect or store personal information such as your email address or precise location on our own servers.

3. Data Storage

Leaderboard information is handled through Meta's platform services and may be stored or processed on Meta's servers in accordance with Meta's applicable privacy policies and practices. The game does not operate its own servers to store leaderboard information.

4. Data Deletion

Information associated with your Meta account is subject to Meta's data management and deletion policies. If you wish to access or delete information associated with your Meta account, please use Meta's account management and privacy tools or contact Meta support.

5. Third-Party Services

Sniper VR may use Meta platform services to provide VR functionality and leaderboard features. Your use of these services is also subject to Meta's applicable terms and privacy policies.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page, and the "Last updated" date will be revised accordingly.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at trueprivacyapp@gmail.com.
